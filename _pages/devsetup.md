My development setup keeps evolving. I work with C++ and Rust, use Linux, and enjoy trying different distributions and desktop environments. This page collects the hardware and tools I use along the way.

## Linux and desktops

I use Ubuntu on the Debian side and Fedora on the RPM side of the Linux world.

On Ubuntu, I recently installed an Omarchy-style setup with Hyprland using Nix. So far, it is not bad! My setup is available in [omarchy-ubuntu-nix](https://github.com/adam-abed-abud/omarchy-ubuntu-nix).

On Fedora, I use GNOME.

## Hardware

I use two Dell laptops:

- **Dell Latitude E-series**.
- **Dell Inspiron 13 5000**.

My mechanical keyboard is a [Keychron V1 8K QMK wired keyboard](https://keychron.ch/products/keychron-v1-8k-qmk-wired-custom-mechanical-keyboard?variant=55885509034369) with Super Banana switches. They have a tactile feel, similar to brown switches.
